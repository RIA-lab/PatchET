Sequence notes for data/opt

The dataset was built from BRENDA (release 2026.1). Every accession in train.csv, val.csv and test.csv is a protein accession listed in BRENDA, and
its EC number and temperature optimum match a BRENDA record for that accession.

However, some of these accessions have since been deleted or demerged in UniProtKB, or are now secondary accessions, so their sequence could not
always be taken from a current UniProtKB entry:

1. ARCHIVED (972 accessions). The UniProtKB entry has been deleted (940) or demerged (32), so no current entry exists. The sequence is the last
   version kept in the UniProt archive (UniSave). These accessions are listed in section A.
2. SECONDARY (75 accessions). The accession is now a secondary accession of another UniProtKB entry. The sequence is that of the current
   primary entry, which is listed next to it in section B.

All other sequences (9,324) are the current UniProtKB sequence of their accession (retrieved 2026-10-05).
